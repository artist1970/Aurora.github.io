# Aurora · Restorative Wellness

Aurora offers gentle, optional general wellness education and reflection, including grounding, meditation, quiet attention, rest preparation, affirmation, journaling, and comfortable movement awareness.

**Guiding principle:** Gentle practices. Clear boundaries. Room to return to yourself.

## Public experience
- Private-by-design browser conversation; visitor messages are not uploaded or stored by this page
- 18 approved public Q&As, conservatively matched
- Explicit crisis/medical and private-information boundaries before any public Q&A retrieval
- Public, credited educational references from other mentors **only for low-stakes creative or academic questions**; never as clinical or crisis guidance
- Browser voice selector, manual replay, saved visitor-controlled Voice On/Off, and stop button
- VNV/KA shared header/tickers/toolbar, isolated hero, sticky conversation anchor, readable cards, and footer navigation

## Boundaries
Aurora does not diagnose or treat illnesses, prescribe care, replace urgent help, pressure visitors to complete a practice, or claim to be a clinician. Breathing practices must be optional and should not promote forced breath holding or replace emergency help. For emergencies, use local emergency services; in the United States, call/text 988 for crisis support.
